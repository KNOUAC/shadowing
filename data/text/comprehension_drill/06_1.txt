Can wisdom in the sense of impartiality be taught? And, if it can, should the teaching of it be one of the aims of education? I should answer both these questions in the affirmative. We are told on Sundays that we should love our neighbour as ourselves. On the other six days of the week, we are exhorted to hate him. It might be protested that it is right to hate those who do harm. I do not think so.
