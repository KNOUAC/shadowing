Perhaps the most striking living example of Christian compassion is Dr. Albert Schweitzer. By the time he was 30 he had acquired fame as scholar, musician and philosopher. Then one day he read an account of the Congo natives and their miserable plight. Since by nature he had to “feel with” them, he had also to take action for them. He abandoned fame and security to spend his life as a doctor among them, sharing their hardships and suffering.
